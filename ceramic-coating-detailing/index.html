The Rodeo Werkz Method

The 6-Step Ceramic Process

Proprietary, repeatable, and documented on every vehicle. This is where the price is justified — and where a coating that lasts is born.

STEP 01

Detail Wash

A full foam-and-hand decontamination wash strips road film and prepares a clean canvas.

STEP 02

Clay Bar Treatment

Bonded contaminants — rail dust, overspray, industrial fallout — are pulled from the paint.

STEP 03

2–3 Step Paint Correction

Swirls, scratches and oxidation are machine-polished out — depth tuned to your paint's condition.

STEP 04

Polish Oil Removal

Every trace of polishing oil is wiped down so the coating bonds to bare, true paint.

STEP 05

Ceramic Coating Installation

The coating is hand-laid in a controlled environment and leveled for a flawless, even cure.

STEP 06

Final Inspection

Inspected under high-output lighting, panel by panel, before your keys are returned.
